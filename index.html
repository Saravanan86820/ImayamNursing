Clinical Procedures at Home

What We Perform

Medical Procedures We Offer

All procedures are performed by certified nurses following clinical protocols and safety guidelines.

Dressing & Injections

Professional wound dressing, IV injections, IM and SC injections administered safely at home with sterile technique.

Catheter Care & Insertion

Urinary catheter insertion, maintenance, and removal following strict aseptic protocol for patient comfort and safety.

Ryles Tube Feeding / Insertion

Nasogastric tube insertion and feeding for patients unable to swallow or consume food orally.

Blood Pressure Monitoring

Regular BP monitoring, recording, and reporting with trend analysis for hypertensive and cardiac patients.

Blood Sugar Testing

Glucometer-based blood sugar testing for diabetic patients with proper documentation and guidance.

Temperature Monitoring

Regular temperature checks and fever monitoring, with documentation and appropriate nursing interventions.

Nebulization

Home nebulization therapy for asthma, COPD, and other respiratory conditions using prescribed medications.

Diaper Care

Professional diaper changing and perineal care for bedridden patients, elderly, and newborns to prevent skin breakdown.

Safety & Hygiene Standards

All procedures are performed using sterile, single-use equipment following hospital-grade infection control protocols. Your safety is our top priority.
